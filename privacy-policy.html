Privacy Policy

Quiz Meister: Wissen & Prüfung

Last updated: June 30, 2026

1 Introduction

Quiz Meister: Wissen & Prüfung ("the App") is developed by AGR. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our App. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2 Data We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and display name when you register
  • Profile data: Username and profile photo (optional)
  • Usage data: Quiz scores, XP points, streaks, and learning progress
  • Game data: Multiplayer game results and friend connections

3 How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To save your learning progress and quiz statistics
  • To enable multiplayer features and friend challenges
  • To display your profile to friends within the app

4 Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Realtime Database). Firebase complies with GDPR and other applicable data protection regulations. Data is stored on servers within the European Union.

5 Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Your username and profile photo are visible only to other users you connect with as friends within the app.

6 Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us at ranaadeem@hotmail.com. We will process your request within 30 days.

7 Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

8 Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to delete your data
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ranaadeem@hotmail.com.
