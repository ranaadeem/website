Methanol-to-Olefins (MTO) for Sustainable Aviation Fuels
Lead researcher for catalyst development and performance evaluation of zeolite-based systems. Designing experimental matrices using DoE to maximise olefin yield. Interfacing with industrial partners for performance benchmarking within the REF4FU sustainable aviation fuel value chain.

Ineratec · OptiSAF · TUBAF · 2023–Present
Hydrotreating & Hydrocracking for SAF Production
Full execution responsibility for lab-scale hydrotreating and hydrocracking of Fischer–Tropsch wax and OGD-derived feeds. Installed and commissioned lab-scale reactor systems; conducted continuous experimental campaigns and catalyst evaluation. Direct interface with industrial partners in project meetings.

Early-stage involvement in catalyst and process development for methanol-to-aromatics conversion targeting petrochemical applications. Contributing to conceptual catalyst design, reaction pathway assessment, and experimental framework establishment.
