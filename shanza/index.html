About Dr. Shanza

Dr. Shanza Ghaffar is an experienced Consultant Obstetrician and Gynaecologist with over 10 years of practice in women's reproductive health across leading public and private hospitals in Lahore. She is proficient in both surgical and non-surgical treatments, including hysterectomy, tubal ligation, and laparotomy, and is known for her holistic approach to patient care, health education, and management of complex gynaecologic and fertility conditions.
