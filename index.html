Research Focus Areas

✈️

Sustainable Aviation Fuels

Methanol-to-Olefins (MTO) using zeolite catalysts and hydrotreating/hydrocracking of Fischer–Tropsch feeds for SAF-range products within the REF4FU and OptiSAF frameworks.

Ongoing · TUBAF · 2023–
⚗️

Heterogeneous Catalysis

Catalyst synthesis, screening, and kinetic evaluation. Zeolite-based systems, metal-supported catalysts, and Design of Experiments for process optimization.

REF4FU · OptiSAF Projects
☀️

Photocatalysis

Modification of graphitic carbon nitride (g-C₃N₄) for redox applications. CO₂ conversion to hydrocarbons. Photocatalytic degradation of persistent organic pollutants.

TUM · PhD Research
🏗️

Zeolite Catalysts & Hydrocarbon Processing

Synthesis and characterisation of zeolite frameworks for selective hydrocarbon conversion. Focus on acidity, porosity, and deactivation behaviour in fixed-bed reactors.

TUBAF · Ongoing
