Biography

I grew up in Pakistan and completed my undergraduate degree in Chemical Engineering at the University of the Punjab in 2010, graduating with a distinction scholarship. My early career took me into industry — I spent nearly three years as an Assistant Manager for R&D at Style Textile in Lahore, working on applied projects for international brands including Nike, Adidas, and Reebok.

In 2013 I moved to Saudi Arabia on a government scholarship to pursue my MS at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM), where I worked on photocatalytic conversion of CO₂ to hydrocarbons — cementing my interest in catalysis as a tool for solving real environmental and energy challenges.

I joined the Technical University of Munich (TUM) in 2017 as a DAAD scholar, completing my PhD (Dr.-Ing.) in 2022 on the modification of graphitic carbon nitride (g-C₃N₄) for redox and hydrogen-related applications. During this period I also taught at UET Lahore and supervised DAAD and Erasmus visiting researchers.

Since August 2023 I have been a Postdoctoral Researcher at TU Bergakademie Freiberg (TUBAF), where my work sits at the heart of the sustainable aviation fuels (SAF) value chain — from methanol-to-olefins using zeolite catalysts to hydrotreating and hydrocracking of Fischer–Tropsch feeds, in collaboration with industrial partners including Ineratec.

Beyond research, I am a passionate photographer and biker. I explore the world through a lens and on two wheels.
