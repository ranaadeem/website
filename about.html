Biography

The Journey

I grew up in Pakistan and completed my undergraduate degree in Chemical Engineering at the University of the Punjab in 2010, graduating with a distinction scholarship. My early career took me into industry — I spent nearly three years as an Assistant Manager for R&D at Style Textile in Lahore, working on applied projects for international brands including Nike, Adidas, and Reebok.

In 2013 I moved to Saudi Arabia on a government scholarship to pursue my MS at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM), where I worked on photocatalytic conversion of CO₂ to hydrocarbons — cementing my interest in catalysis as a tool for solving real environmental and energy challenges.

I joined the Technical University of Munich (TUM) in 2017 as a DAAD scholar, completing my PhD (Dr.-Ing.) in 2022 on the modification of graphitic carbon nitride (g-C₃N₄) for redox and hydrogen-related applications. During this period I also taught at UET Lahore and supervised DAAD and Erasmus visiting researchers.

Since August 2023 I have been a Postdoctoral Researcher at TU Bergakademie Freiberg (TUBAF), where my work sits at the heart of the sustainable aviation fuels (SAF) value chain — from methanol-to-olefins using zeolite catalysts to hydrotreating and hydrocracking of Fischer–Tropsch feeds, in collaboration with industrial partners including Ineratec.

Beyond research, I am a passionate photographer and biker, exploring the world through a lens and on two wheels.

About Me

Chemical Engineer · Researcher · Educator

I'm a chemical engineer with a passion for understanding how materials interact at the molecular level to drive meaningful chemical transformations. My work centres on heterogeneous catalysis — developing, modifying, and testing catalysts for sustainable processes, from photocatalytic water splitting to zeolite-based methanol conversion for sustainable aviation fuels.

My academic journey has taken me from Lahore to Dhahran to Munich to Freiberg — each move shaped not just my research but my perspective. I completed my BS with distinction at the University of the Punjab, earned a Saudi Government scholarship for my MS at KFUPM, and received a DAAD scholarship for my PhD at TU Munich, where I spent five years modifying graphitic carbon nitride for redox and photocatalytic applications. Since 2023, I've been a Postdoctoral Researcher at TU Bergakademie Freiberg, working on MTO and SAF-related projects within industry-academic consortia.

Beyond the lab, I care deeply about education. I taught core chemical engineering courses at UET Pakistan — first as a Lecturer (2015–2017), then briefly as an Assistant Professor (2023) — and supervised undergraduate thesis projects. At TUM, I mentored DAAD and Erasmus exchange students, helping them define research objectives and build hands-on laboratory skills.

I believe science should be visible, accessible, and human. On this site I share my research, publications, and occasional writing on academic life, navigating Germany as a Pakistani researcher, and everything in between.
